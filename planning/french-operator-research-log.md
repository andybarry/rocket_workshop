# Research Log — French Tour Operator Planning a USA STEM Tour

*Persona exercise · September 2026*

**Purpose:** Simulate the research a program manager at a French educational
tour operator performs when building a USA STEM tour for high-school
students (lycéens), and log every action taken. The goal is to map where
these buyers actually look, so Stage One can later figure out how to be
found by them.

**Persona:** "Responsable production" at a mid-size French school-travel
operator (the SILC / CEI / Verdié tier). Task: design a sellable
"séjour scientifique aux États-Unis" for ~40 lycéens plus chaperones.

---

## Part 1 — The action log

Each step records what was searched, why, what came back, and the decision
it produced. Steps 1–4 ran in French (how this person starts); the rest in
English (where they inevitably end up).

### Step 1 — Orientation search (French)

- **Action:** Web search: `voyage scolaire scientifique États-Unis lycée programme`
- **Intent:** See what already exists on the French market before designing anything.
- **Found:** Only French operators — [SILC](https://silc.fr/voyages-scolaires/destinations-voyages-scolaires/etats-unis-voyages-scolaires/), [CEI Voyage](https://www.cei-voyage.fr/destination/voyager-etats-unis/), [Nacel](https://www.nacel.fr/sejour-longue-duree/etats-unis/scolarite-a-l-etranger/scolarite-etats-unis), [La Ligue de l'enseignement](https://www.sejours-educatifs.org/vv/voyage-scolaire/pays-etats-unis-6/), [ECI](https://www.eci.asso.fr/voyages-scolaires/etats-unis/). All generalist language/culture trips (NYC, Washington). **No science-themed USA product on page one.**
- **Decision:** There is a gap in the French market. Check a competitor's catalog to confirm.

### Step 2 — Keyword variation (French)

- **Action:** Web search: `séjour scolaire STEM USA lycéens sciences ingénierie`
- **Intent:** Does "STEM" as a keyword lead anywhere in French?
- **Found:** Long-stay exchange programs ([PIE](https://www.piefrance.com/destinations/usa/sejours-scolaires-de-longue-duree-aux-usa/)), a [Wikipedia definition of STEM](https://fr.wikipedia.org/wiki/STEM_(disciplines)), an [EducationUSA/Fulbright PDF](https://fulbright-france.org/sites/default/files/stem_2025.pdf.pdf). Nothing bookable for a group trip.
- **Decision:** "STEM" is not yet a productive French search term for group travel. The French web dead-ends; competitor catalog check next, then switch to English.

### Step 3 — Competitor catalog review (French)

- **Action:** Fetched [SILC's USA school-trips page](https://silc.fr/voyages-scolaires/destinations-voyages-scolaires/etats-unis-voyages-scolaires/).
- **Intent:** Benchmark the standard product and its price.
- **Found:** Washington 10 days from €1,562; New York 7 days from €900–1,054; combined NY+DC 10 days from €1,250 (all excluding transatlantic transport). Themes: language immersion, history, institutions. **Zero science/STEM content.**
- **Decision:** Price anchor established (~€1,000–1,600 land-only). A STEM tour can be premium-priced if it has real hands-on content. Now source that content.

### Step 4 — Anchor attraction check (French)

- **Action:** Web search: `visite NASA groupe scolaire français Kennedy Space Center lycée`
- **Intent:** French teachers ask for NASA by name — verify it's doable for a group.
- **Found:** [Kennedy Space Center visitor info](https://frenchdistrict.com/floride/articles/kennedy-space-center-titusville-lancement-fusees/), French-speaking guide services, French audio guides available. School groups routinely visit.
- **Decision:** KSC works as a Florida option. But Florida is far from the classic East Coast circuit — hold as an extension, not the core.

### Step 5 — Switch to English: find the US model

- **Action:** Web search: `STEM educational tour USA high school student groups itinerary`
- **Intent:** See how US operators structure a STEM tour (itinerary skeleton to copy).
- **Found:** The US student-travel industry: [WorldStrides STEM programs](https://worldstrides.com/en-us/teachers/programs-and-tours/stem-science/stem-programs-us), [Educational Travel Adventures](https://www.etadventures.com/educational-tours/subjects/stem/), [EA School Tours](https://www.easchooltours.com/subjects/stem), [YNI Tours](https://www.ynitours.com/usa-stem-field-trip). Standard skeleton: Boston tech corridor or Silicon Valley, Kennedy Space Center / Space Camp, 4–6 day formats.
- **Decision:** Boston is the STEM capital for a first product. Investigate Boston components.

### Step 6 — Boston campus visits

- **Action:** Web search: `MIT Harvard campus tour school groups Boston science field trip`
- **Intent:** Lock the two "wow" names every French parent recognizes.
- **Found:** [Trademark Tours](https://trademarktours.com/) runs official Harvard/MIT tours; MIT waives tour fees for educator bookings; [Museum of Science](https://www.mos.org/teaching-stem/field-trips) flagged for hands-on STEM.
- **Decision:** MIT + Harvard walking tours confirmed as free/cheap daytime anchors. Still missing: actual *doing* (building, coding) rather than visiting.

### Step 7 — The hands-on gap

- **Action:** Web search: `hands-on engineering workshop delivered to student group hotel Boston robotics AI`
- **Intent:** Find a provider who brings a build-something experience to the group (evenings are dead time in the itinerary; venue transport is expensive).
- **Found:** [MassRobotics educational visits](https://www.massrobotics.org/stem/educational-visits-and-workshops/) (1–2h workshops at their Seaport facility), summer programs (Putney, BU RASTIC) that don't fit touring groups. **No provider offering delivery to the group's hotel appeared.**
- **Decision:** MassRobotics is a viable daytime visit. The in-hotel evening slot remains unfilled — keep looking.

### Step 8 — Evening activity search

- **Action:** Web search: `STEM workshop evening activity for student tour group hotel USA`
- **Intent:** Directly attack the evening-programming gap.
- **Found:** [EF Explore America STEM Discovery Boston](https://www.efexploreamerica.com/educational-tour/stem-boston) and [NYC](https://www.efexploreamerica.com/educational-tour/stem-new-york-city) tours (packaged, US-school-facing), [iFLY STEM field trips](https://www.iflyworld.com/programs/stem-field-trips) (2–4h, can run after hours). No standalone in-hotel workshop provider on page one.
- **Decision:** EF's packaging confirms evening STEM slots are a recognized product feature — but as a French operator I can't buy EF's product; I need the underlying suppliers.

### Step 9 — Following the supplier trail

- **Action:** Fetched [WorldStrides' US STEM page](https://worldstrides.com/en-us/teachers/programs-and-tours/stem-science/stem-programs-us); then searched `robotics workshop "MIT PhD" student tour groups hotel venue`.
- **Intent:** WorldStrides advertises "robotics workshops led by MIT PhD graduates" — find who actually delivers that.
- **Found:** The search surfaced MIT academic pages, [Four Winds Tours' robotics tour](https://fourwindstours.com/tour-posts/robotics-tour/), MassRobotics again. **The actual workshop supplier behind the WorldStrides line was not findable by search.**
- **Decision:** Suppliers of this exact service are invisible; they appear to be reached only through operator partnerships, not search. Note for negotiation: whoever they are, they have no direct channel to me.

### Step 10 — Last attempt at the workshop supplier, then venue logistics

- **Action:** Web searches: `engineering workshops delivered to hotels student travel groups instructor equipment included` and `Museum of Science Boston group visit school groups reservation`.
- **Intent:** One more phrasing for the hotel-delivered workshop; start locking venue reservations.
- **Found:** First query: only tour packagers ([Educational Destinations](https://www.educationaldestinations.com/), [The Learning Adventure](https://thelearningadventure.com/en-us/subjects/sciences/) — UK-based, offers a rocket-building workshop abroad) — again **no direct US in-hotel provider**. Second query: [Museum of Science group booking](https://www.mos.org/visit/faq) — groups line: 617-589-3109 / groups@mos.org, book well ahead.
- **Decision:** Give up finding the in-hotel supplier by search; will ask the receptive agency (step 12) who they use. Museum of Science booked as a half-day.

### Step 11 — Washington & New York components + entry formalities

- **Action:** Web searches: `Smithsonian Air and Space Museum school group reservation timed entry`; `Intrepid Museum New York school group visit MoMath field trip`; `ESTA groupe scolaire voyage États-Unis élèves mineurs formalités`.
- **Intent:** Confirm the other cities' STEM anchors and the French-side paperwork.
- **Found:** [Air & Space](https://airandspace.si.edu/visit/museum-dc/guidelines) needs group reservations months ahead (free timed entry); [Intrepid Museum](https://intrepidmuseum.org/plan-your-visit/group-visits) 60-min school programs, book 2+ weeks out; [MoMath school groups](https://momath.org/school-groups/) pre-K–12. Formalities: individual passports + ESTA for every minor, AST (autorisation de sortie du territoire), the French embassy's mandatory school-travel declaration platform ([Académie de Paris guidance](https://www.ac-paris.fr/deplacements-scolaires-a-l-etranger-formalites-130314)).
- **Decision:** NYC and DC anchors confirmed. Formalities are standard — build an 8-week paperwork lead into the sales calendar.

### Step 12 — The buying channel (French, B2B)

- **Action:** Web searches: `agence réceptive États-Unis groupes scolaires français DMC voyage éducatif`; `vol groupe scolaire Paris Boston tarif groupe compagnie aérienne réservation`.
- **Intent:** How do I actually contract all this? French operators buy US ground services through francophone receptive agencies (DMCs), and group air through dedicated desks.
- **Found:** DMC directories [receptifs.com](https://receptifs.com/etats-unis.html) and [KAPYTO](https://www.kapyto.com/amerique-du-nord/etats-unis.html); [Réceptif Voyages](https://www.receptifvoyages.com/) (francophone USA/Canada group DMC); the [UNOSEL](https://www.unosel.org/organismes-voyages-scolaires/) label directory on the French side. Group air: Air France group desk negotiates school fares CDG–BOS; brokers like [Monde du Voyage](https://www.monde-du-voyage.com/vol-eurowings-groupe-scolaire.php) and [Bourse des Vols](https://www.bourse-des-vols.com/voyages-groupes.php) specialize in school groups.
- **Decision:** Product is assemblable. Hand the ground program to a francophone DMC, ask them explicitly for a hands-on engineering workshop supplier for the hotel evenings.

## Part 3 — What this exercise reveals (how to find these people)

1. **The French-language search space is an empty beachhead.** Queries
   like *voyage scolaire scientifique États-Unis* return only generalist
   French operators and zero STEM content. French-language pages targeting
   these phrases (Stage One's site already has full French i18n) would
   face almost no competition.

2. **Stage One is invisible for its own core queries.** Across every
   phrasing of its exact offer — "hands-on engineering workshop delivered
   to student group hotel," "STEM workshop evening activity tour group,"
   "engineering workshops delivered to hotels instructor equipment
   included" — stageoneeducation.com never appeared in results. The buyer
   persona could not find it by searching, and gave up (steps 7–10).

3. **The buyers are enumerable, not searchable.** The realistic path to
   these people is direct outreach, because their procurement runs through
   a small, listable set of intermediaries:
   - **French operators:** SILC, CEI, Nacel, Verdié, La Ligue de
     l'enseignement, ECI — and the whole [UNOSEL member directory](https://www.unosel.org/organismes-voyages-scolaires/).
   - **Francophone receptive DMCs for the USA:** the agencies listed on
     receptifs.com and KAPYTO, Réceptif Voyages — these are who a French
     operator will *ask* for an evening workshop supplier.
   - **US packagers already selling the concept:** WorldStrides literally
     advertises "robotics workshops led by MIT PhD graduates," EF sells
     evening-inclusive STEM Boston tours — partnership channels where the
     hotel-delivered workshop is a known product need.

4. **The evening slot is the recognized, unfilled gap.** The persona's
   itinerary had three dead hotel evenings and no searchable supplier to
   fill them. That is Stage One's exact product; the demand exists (EF and
   WorldStrides package it), but independent operators and DMCs can't find
   a direct supplier.

5. **Content that would capture this buyer at each step:** a French-language
   "séjour scientifique USA" landing page (steps 1–2); a sample 9-day East
   Coast STEM itinerary page in English and French (step 5); pages
   targeting "evening activity for student tour groups in Boston /
   Washington / Orlando" (steps 7–8); and a listing or partnership presence
   in the DMC directories where the ground program actually gets bought
   (step 12).
