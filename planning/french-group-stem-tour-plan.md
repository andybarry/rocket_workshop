## Workshop options

Each workshop is a complete three-hour program delivered to the group's
venue — Stage One brings the instructor, equipment, materials, and
curriculum, and handles setup and cleanup. No prior experience is required.

**Artificial Intelligence** — Students train neural networks, work
hands-on with text, image, and music models, and close with a structured
discussion of AI ethics and bias.

**Robotics (Drone)** — Students build, program, test, and fly a working
drone system: electronics and microcontroller wiring, coding and
troubleshooting, and programming autonomous flight. All electronics are
low voltage and safety equipment is provided.

**Mechanical Engineering** — Every student designs a complete multi-part
model rocket in professional CAD software and sees how designs become
physical objects through 3D printing. No prior CAD experience needed.

*(Web Engineering — build and publish a live website — is coming soon and
can be discussed for later programs.)*

For a multi-day tour, running two or three different workshops on separate
evenings gives the group a progression across engineering disciplines
without repeating content.

## Working with a French-speaking group

- Workshops are instructed in English. For a French group this doubles as
  an authentic English-immersion experience — the format is hands-on and
  visual, so the build itself carries students past vocabulary gaps.
- Instructors adjust pace and technical depth to the group; chaperones and
  accompanying teachers can support translation where helpful.
- stageoneeducation.com has a full French translation (language switcher →
  Français), so teachers, parents, and the tour provider can review the
  program in French before the trip.
- No prior engineering experience is expected on any workshop.

## Scheduling model

The three-hour block can run morning, afternoon, or evening. For touring
groups the strongest fit is usually the **evening slot at the group's
hotel**: daytime stays free for sightseeing and institutional visits, and
hotel downtime becomes a highlight of the trip. Arrival-day afternoons
also work well, since the workshop requires no transportation once the
group is at the venue.

### Option B — East Coast, Boston + New Haven + Washington, D.C.

| Segment | Program |

| New Haven (1 day) | Yale campus visit; **workshop 2** at venue |

The workshop format is identical in every city, so the program stays
consistent as the group travels.

Other delivery cities available if the itinerary differs: Ann Arbor,
Atlanta, Berkeley, Denver, Houston, Los Angeles, Orlando, San Francisco.

## Venue and logistics requirements

- Any hotel meeting room, classroom, or group venue works — no lab needed.
- Tables and chairs for the group, plus standard power outlets.
- Stage One delivers all equipment and materials and handles setup and
  cleanup; the group needs no preparation.
- One point of contact at Stage One coordinates dates, venue requirements,
  group size, and workshop selection with the tour provider.

## Planning checklist

1. **Confirm with the tour provider:** destinations, dates, group size,
   ages, and hotel/venue for each city.
2. **Select workshops:** which of the three programs, and how many
   sessions across the itinerary.
3. **Reserve the slots:** lock the evening/arrival-day blocks into the
   tour itinerary and confirm the meeting room at each hotel.
4. **Share the French-language pages** with the group's teachers so
   students and parents know what to expect.
5. **Reconfirm one week out:** final headcount, venue room name, and
   arrival time for setup.

## Résumé en français (à partager avec le groupe)

Stage One Education propose des ateliers d'ingénierie de trois heures,
animés par de vrais ingénieurs et livrés directement à l'hôtel ou au lieu
d'accueil du groupe — matériel, équipement et programme inclus. Aucune
expérience préalable n'est requise et l'encadrement s'adapte du collège au
lycée.

**Les ateliers :**

- **Intelligence artificielle** — entraîner de vrais réseaux de neurones,
  utiliser les derniers modèles d'IA (texte, image, musique) et débattre
  de l'éthique de l'IA.
- **Robotique (drone)** — construire, câbler, programmer et faire voler un
  drone, jusqu'au vol autonome.
- **Génie mécanique** — concevoir une fusée complète en CAO
  professionnelle et découvrir l'impression 3D.

Les ateliers se déroulent en anglais dans un format très visuel et
pratique — une véritable immersion linguistique où chaque élève construit,
code et teste quelque chose de réel. Le site
[stageoneeducation.com](https://stageoneeducation.com) est disponible en
français (sélecteur de langue → Français).

Généralement programmés en soirée à l'hôtel du groupe, les ateliers
transforment le temps libre en un moment fort du voyage, sans rien retirer
aux visites de la journée.
