Share what you know about your group, destination, and schedule. An exact date or final group size is not required to begin planning.

Select the workshop experiences that interest your group.

What happens next

After you send these details, your email application will open a message to the Stage One team. Once that message is sent, the team will review your destination, group details, schedule, and workshop interests and follow up with next steps.

Stage One brings the instructor, equipment, materials, and curriculum directly to your hotel, school, campus, or group venue. No previous experience is required.
