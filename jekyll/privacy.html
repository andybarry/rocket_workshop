Privacy Policy

Stage One Education, LLC collects the information you choose to share when you plan a workshop. That may include your name, work email, phone number, organization, group details, destination, schedule, and workshop interests.

The planning form opens a message in your email application addressed to workshops@stageoneeducation.com. Stage One uses that information to respond to your inquiry and coordinate a workshop. Personal information is not placed in website URLs.

We do not sell this information. Analytics events on the website track navigation choices such as region or workshop interest and do not include names or email addresses.

Questions about this policy can be sent to workshops@stageoneeducation.com.
