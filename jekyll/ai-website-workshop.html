Web Development Workshop

{% include workshop-plan-entry.html slug="web-development" %}

3-3.5 Hours | All Ages | No Experience Needed

Participants turn an idea into a live website while learning the foundations of layout, content, design, and front-end development. By the end of the workshop, they have created a real digital project they can publish and share.

Stage One brings the instructor, equipment, materials, and curriculum directly to the group’s hotel, school, campus, or venue. No previous engineering or coding experience is required. The workshop can be adapted for middle school, high school, college, adult, and mixed-age groups.

  • Website layout and front-end development
  • HTML, CSS, and AI-assisted coding
  • Publishing a live website
