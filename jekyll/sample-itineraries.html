Sample STEM Tour Itineraries

Every Stage One workshop is a complete three-hour program — instructor, equipment, materials, and curriculum included — delivered to the group's hotel, school, campus, or venue. These sample itineraries show where tour planners typically schedule the workshop: in the evening or arrival-day slots that sightseeing can't fill, with no transportation and no lab required. Use them as a starting point — Stage One coordinates the workshop around your program, not the other way around.
