      Ann Arbor MI Engineering Workshops

      Ann Arbor's academic culture, engineering community, and focus on research make it a natural destination for STEM-oriented travel. A Stage One workshop adds a substantial hands-on component after an institutional visit or during an open hotel block, with instructors, equipment, and materials included.
