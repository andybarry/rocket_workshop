      We're dedicated to sharing our love of engineering

      Our workshops answer questions like,

      can I do engineering?

      what do engineers really do?

      through hands-on activities—from building real circuits to working with professional-grade CAD tools and the latest AI models—participants gain a true sense of what engineering is all about.

      After engaging with real-world projects, engineering tools, and talking to experienced professionals, participants walk away more confident, inspired, and excited about their potential future in engineering.

      Stage One Education Instructors Nationwide network of passionate engineering professionals

      Leadership Team

      Andrew Barry, MIT Ph.D.
      Founder
      Andy fell in love with autonomous drones by flying them for his Ph.D. at MIT. Andy has brought electronics and software workshops to over 10,000 students, from small helicopters to sophisticated quadrupeds.
      Pete Florence, MIT Ph.D.
      Co-Founder
      Pete flew drones of all sorts during his Ph.D. work at MIT. His planning and control work in the Robot Locomotion Group pushed the horizons for faster, more agile, and more reliable drones. His award-winning robot manipulation research is giving robots new abilities every day.
      Robbie Kipp, Engineer
      Director of Operations
      Robbie's engineering designs set the course for the future of dive systems and maritime vessels during his time as an ocean engineer and naval architect. Now, as the Director of Operations at Stage One Education, he pioneers innovative approaches to inspire the next generation of engineers. Robbie is an avid diver, climber, and aviation enthusiast.

      Workshop Details

      • Workshops Offered: Robotics, AI, Mechanical Engineering
      • Length: 3 hours
      • Participants: Up to ~60 per workshop (venue size determines capacity)
      • Location: We can accommodate any location
      • Venue: Must include Wi-Fi, power outlets, tables, and chairs. larger spaces preferred
      • Materials: Included with all workshops
      • Instructors: Experienced engineering professionals who are passionate about sharing their expertise.
      • Schedule: We can work around your itentrary and have the capacity to host multiple or simultaneous sessions as needed

      Pricing

      The per participant cost is determined by factors such as the group size, the scheduling of consecutive workshop days, asset management, and the instructors provided.
