      About Us
      We're dedicated to sharing our love of engineering

      Our workshops answer questions like,

      can I do engineering?

      what do engineers really do?

      through hands-on activities—from building real circuits to working with professional-grade CAD tools and the latest AI models—participants gain a true sense of what engineering is all about.

      After engaging with real-world projects, engineering tools, and talking to experienced professionals, participants walk away more confident, inspired, and excited about their potential future in engineering.

      • Instructors: Experienced engineering professionals who are passionate about sharing their expertise.
      • Schedule: We can work around your itentrary and have the capacity to host multiple or simultaneous sessions as needed
